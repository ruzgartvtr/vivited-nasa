# Vivited — Promotional Video Script (English)

**Length:** about 1 minute 45 seconds · **Speakers:** six students  
**Product name:** Vivited (never Marswalk / ARES on camera)  
**Only describe features that exist in the finished Flutter web app.**

---

## Shot plan (suggested)

1. Opening title + orbit view  
2. Map with Habitat H0 / checkpoints  
3. Layer switches (imagery → relief → hazard)  
4. Routes A–D in Plan  
5. Life-support simulator + warning  
6. Closing line + Sources labels  

---

## Script

### Speaker 1 — Introduction (~15 s)

Hi — we’re team Vivited. For the NASA Space Apps Challenge we built **Vivited**, a Flutter web map that asks one question: how can astronauts explore Mars safely, check earlier robotic observations, and collect new science — on a single interactive map of Jezero crater?

### Speaker 2 — Jezero exploration (~15 s)

Jezero held an ancient lake. Perseverance has been walking this ground since 2021. In Vivited we mark a simulated Habitat **H0** at Three Forks, plus terrain, validation, discovery and safety checkpoints — fiction for the habitat role, real NASA coordinates for the places.

### Speaker 3 — NASA data layers (~20 s)

We combine three data families. Optical CTX imagery shows the surface. The twenty-metre stereo elevation model gives slopes and route profiles. Perseverance MEDA climatology shows historical temperature, pressure and wind by season — not live weather. Every layer is labelled NASA DATA, scientific interpretation, or simulation.

### Speaker 4 — Four mission routes (~20 s)

Judges can compare four educational routes. Route A is safe access toward the fan front. Route B validates early robotic geology on the crater floor. Route C goes to Bright Angel for a discovery question. Route D is the return corridor home to Habitat H0. Distances, climb and duration come from the terrain model — these are planning corridors, not NASA-approved paths.

### Speaker 5 — Oxygen and energy simulation (~20 s)

Life support has two honest layers: a planning model tied to the walk, and a clear teaching simulator. Starting oxygen one hundred, one hundred twenty minutes at zero point three per minute leaves sixty-four — reserve satisfied. Stretch the mission and Vivited warns: Return to Habitat / Reduce Mission Time. It is an educational simulation — not for real astronaut operations.

### Speaker 6 — Functional demonstration + close (~15 s)

No login. Open Vivited in a browser, press Demo, and follow fifteen steps across the real interface — map, layers, routes, simulator, sources. We learned that exploring Mars safely means reading terrain, science and resources together — and saying clearly where the data ends.

**End card:** Vivited · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Jezero crater

- Keep the educational disclaimer visible when the simulator warning appears.
